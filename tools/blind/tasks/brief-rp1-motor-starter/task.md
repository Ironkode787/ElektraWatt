# Brief RP1: a three-phase motor starter

A customer wants a direct-on-line starter for a three-phase motor on a machine: a contactor in the control cabinet
switches the motor's three phases. You are asked to draw it in the new project that is already open, and to hand over
the drawing.

Run `{run}` is a `{kind}` run with the caps {turns} turns, {steps} steps, {looks} looks and {refusals} refused steps.

## The situation

- The mains arrive in the cabinet on a terminal strip: the three phases L1, L2 and L3 and the protective earth PE.
- The contactor and the motor are among the program's generic starter parts.

## What to draw

- Folio 101: the mains terminals. Each of L1, L2, L3 and PE continues to the starter's page through an interruption
  point of that name.
- Folio 102, the motor starter, on one page: L1, L2, L3 and PE arrive at interruption points at the page's left edge.
  Each phase goes through its own main contact of the contactor, in phase order, to the motor's U1, V1 and W1; PE goes
  to the motor's PE.
- Let the program draw the routes of the starter page's wires (Route page), and look at what it drew: say in your
  notes where a wire runs as you would draw it and where it does not.

## What to hand over

- The project, saved.
- A PDF of the drawing, exported by you.

Use placeholder data only: no real names, addresses or drawing numbers. Which tags you give is up to you, as you would
do it for a customer.
