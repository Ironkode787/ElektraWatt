# The free task

There is no job sheet: you choose the job. Draw a small control-cabinet circuit of your own in the new project that is
already open, as you would for a customer who has asked for a motor starter, a lamp circuit, a heater with a thermostat
or any other small circuit of your trade.

- Use placeholder data only: tags such as `-101K1`, texts such as "Motor starter", no real names, addresses or drawing
  numbers.
- Make at least one folio with several devices, wires between them and one terminal strip.
- Look at your work with the page description as you go, and put right what differs from what you meant.
- When you are satisfied, **save the project and export the PDF** (name the file `set.pdf`). Both are your own steps.

Run `{run}` is a `{kind}` run with the caps {turns} turns, {steps} steps, {looks} looks and {refusals} refused steps.
The project was made with the company standard of the wizard; there is no folio of yours yet.

Say in your notes what you set out to draw and why, and how close you got. There is no score: the people who read your run
want to learn where the program helped you and where it did not.
