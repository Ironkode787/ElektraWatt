# Task T2: wire a circuit breaker

You are working on an existing project for a control cabinet, which is already open. On folio 102 a colleague has just
put the miniature circuit breaker back between the incoming 24 V and the strip -X1021, but it has no wires yet.

Run `{run}` is a `{kind}` run with the caps {turns} turns, {steps} steps, {looks} looks and {refusals} refused steps.

## The job

Connect the breaker's input to the incoming 24 V, and its output to terminal 1 of -X1021.

- The 24 V comes in at the left edge of folio 102 from the power supply on folio 101; it goes to input 1 of the breaker.
- Output 2 of the breaker goes to terminal 1 of -X1021.
- When you are done, none of these connection points is left unconnected.

Leave the project saved when you are done.
