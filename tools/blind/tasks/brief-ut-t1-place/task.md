# Task T1: place a circuit breaker

You are working on an existing project for a control cabinet, which is already open. On folio 102 the control supply
lost its circuit breaker: the 24 V power supply -102G1 at the top of the folio feeds nothing, and the load
distribution -102F2 below it has no input.

Run `{run}` is a `{kind}` run with the caps {turns} turns, {steps} steps, {looks} looks and {refusals} refused steps.

## The job

Add the 16 A miniature circuit breaker A9F78116 to folio 102, between the power supply and the load distribution.

- The breaker is a 1-pole miniature circuit breaker, part number A9F78116 (Schneider Electric, 16 A, characteristic B).
- It sits on the drawing between -102G1 and -102F2, as a breaker between a supply and its loads would.
- Give it the tag the project's numbering gives it; do not type a tag of your own.
- You do not need to wire it: that is another job.

Leave the project saved when you are done.
