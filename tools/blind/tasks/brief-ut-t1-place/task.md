# Task T1: place a circuit breaker

You are working on an existing project for a control cabinet, which is already open. On folio 102 the control supply
lost its circuit breaker: the 24 V that comes in from folio 101 at the left edge feeds nothing, and the 24 V strip
-X1021 has no input.

Run `{run}` is a `{kind}` run with the caps {turns} turns, {steps} steps, {looks} looks and {refusals} refused steps.

## The job

Add the miniature circuit breaker EW-GEN-MCB-1 to folio 102, between the incoming 24 V and the strip -X1021.

- The breaker is a single-pole miniature circuit breaker, part number EW-GEN-MCB-1 (the generic parts of the program).
- It sits on the drawing between the 24 V at the left edge and -X1021, as a breaker between a supply and its loads would.
- Give it the tag the project's numbering gives it; do not type a tag of your own.
- You do not need to wire it: that is another job.

Leave the project saved when you are done.
