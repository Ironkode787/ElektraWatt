# M3: automation breadth

**Status:** running · **Started:** 2026-11-13 · **Base:** `0926a57ce` (0.2.0 is `v0.2.0`) · **Release:** 0.3.0

M3 is new development only. It builds the 1.0 layer of the PLC, bus and fluid models on top of the MVP layer that
0.1.0 shipped, the exchange with PLC software, the EDZ, EPLAN parts, DXF and DWG imports, and the automation
workspaces of the window. The design documents are the specification; doc 20's gate G-M3 is the automated half of
the definition of done.

**The gate** is the owner's own hands-on test of a candidate of 0.3.0. A persona round of three or four runs comes
before it. A passing suite is not the gate.

## How a work package is built

1. **One brief, one lane.** Each work package is built by one agent in its own worktree, from a brief that names
   the design sections, the requirements, the acceptance criteria and the rows of [backlog.md](backlog.md) it takes.
   The model and its effort are chosen for the work.
2. **The design document is the specification.** Where it leaves a choice, the lane decides and reports the
   decision. Where it is wrong or out of date, the lane corrects the document in the same change.
3. **Must and Should.** The work package's requirements of priority *Must* and *Should* are built; *Could* goes to
   Later (above).
4. **The format grows additively** under ADR-0011 amendment 6: an optional member without cascade semantics, a new
   variant caught by `Unknown`, a new record kind that older readers keep verbatim. `cargo xtask api-guard` says
   0 breaking. A member that an older reader would have to honour to keep the file consistent (a frozen address,
   an ownership) needs a new format version: the lane stops on it and reports, and M3 takes at most one such step.
5. **Reachable.** At the exit every feature can be reached from the window or from `ew`. The work packages of
   models and exchange build commands, derivations, rules, reports and the `ew` entry; WP-S2.13 builds the
   window's surfaces for them. The import work packages extend the window's own import flow themselves.
6. **Tested where it is used.** A behaviour gets its test at the level a person uses it: a command, an `ew` run, or
   the window's pointer and keys. `cargo xtask check -p` on the crates a change reaches.
7. **Reviewed.** Every M3 work package touches the format or a core crate, so each gets one independent review.
   The reviewer fixes its own minor findings; a major goes back to the lane. A fix is not reviewed again.

  safety, freeze, ownership and exchange, about thirty documented rules, eleven reports, every exchange format, the
  DXF reader and writer. The rows of [backlog-later.md](backlog-later.md) that belong to a work package moved to
  [backlog.md](backlog.md).
- **WP-S1.24 merged (2026-11-13).** Seven commits: the 1.0 members of the PLC and bus records, the tag-based
  profile `rockwell-logix`, `plc.freeze`, `plc.readdress` and `plc.convert_manual_to_auto`, traced targets,
  automatic symbols, the process-tag scheme, twenty rules of the EW-PLC, EW-SAF, EW-PRC, EW-BUS and EW-IOL packs
  with the prevent halves of EW-PLC-001, -007 and -011, six lists with the generated bus topology overview, and the
