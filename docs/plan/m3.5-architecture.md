## 1. The rule every lane keeps

1. **No behaviour changes.** A lane moves and merges. What the program does, says and stores is the same before
   and after, and the tests that pin it pass unchanged; a test that pinned two copies equal goes with the copy.
2. **Two copies that differ become two named forms.** Where the copies of one thing give different results, the
   one home holds both forms side by side under names that say the difference (`slot_text` and `slot_short`), and
   each caller keeps the form it had. Making them one is the release manager's decision or a row of the backlog; a
   lane never picks.
3. **A move is committed apart** from anything else.
4. **A replaced thing is removed in the same change,** from every caller: a lane that gives a thing its home
   switches all callers, in whichever crate they are, and deletes the copies.
5. **A text keeps its English byte for byte** when it moves onto the path of the architecture's §18, and its
   Dutch is written in the same change.
6. **A lane that finds its row would change behaviour stops at that row,** leaves it out and reports it; the
   release manager decides it, or it becomes a row of the backlog.

## 2. Decided and not built

Release manager, by the owner's word that such questions are his to decide (intuitive for the user, easy to
maintain):

- A rule's six-digit code stays as each rule declares it ([15](../design/15-rule-engine.md) §2.2: a designed number
  that the message panel prints and the findings sort by).
- The lists keep their refill loop (`ew-reports/src/paginate.rs`).
- The command parameters' schema types stay in `ew-commands/src/proxy.rs`, because sharing them renames entries of
  the published schemas.
- A toast's button keeps its own short word and is not labelled by its action's menu text, and a toned toast's
  title stays the part of its text before the first `": "` (ADR-0035 amendment 3). An offer of a toast runs with
  its own parameters.
- The window's commit and the headless one stay two named forms (`ShellModel::commit`, `run_logged`), as do the two
  preview-then-commit helpers, because each records and words a refusal its own way.
- The describe text and the state's JSON of a headless run stay two forms (`ew-viewmodel/src/describe.rs`,
  `script.rs`): each prints other members in other units, and one view for both changes bytes of every transcript.
- A refused preview's card keeps the command error's words.
- Of the shell's long functions `resolve_at`, `act_macros_save` and `station_boxes` stay whole: their steps would
  share fourteen or more locals.
- The window's events that call a setter of the shell stay events (the palette's typing, a form's fields, a toast's
  Close and timeout): as actions each would add a line to every run's transcript, and none is an act a script
  needs.
- The prelude of `brief-ut-t6-timed-circuit` keeps its three embedded part copies: they are copies of the sample
  parts library, and an action that embeds a library part by its number would exist for this one prelude.
- The table of settable members (`ew-commands/src/commands/fields.rs` `SETTABLE`) stays: its line decides that a
  member may be set.
- The size ceiling is the one of `xtask/src/ceiling.rs`; at 2,000 lines thirty files would be split.

## 3. The second scan

Before the candidate the whole repository was read again on the tree after the last stage and the owner's items:
eight readers, one per region of the first scan and none a lane that built it, asked what the first scan found and
the rework missed, what is still in more than one place and where the code differs from the accepted architecture.
They listed 164 findings. The release manager triaged them in one sitting; those that change no behaviour, and the
decisions he took, were mended in four lanes (three of code, one of the documents), and the rest are rows of the backlog
with their reasons: FT-2174 to FT-2198 in [the backlog](backlog.md), "M3.5: what its own steps leave", and
FT-1712 in [the later backlog](backlog-later.md). The review of step 14 reads the fix lanes' commits with the rest.
