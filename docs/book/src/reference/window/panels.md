# Panels

The window's panes sit around the page view: the navigators on the left, the property grid on the right, and the
find pane, the jobs, the preview, the history and the glossary in the bottom dock. View ▸ Panels shows and hides them.
Help ▸ Documentation in a focused pane opens its section here. The keys of each pane are on the
[keymap](../keymaps.md) page.

## Panes

### Page view

The canvas: the open pages as tabs, one or two groups side by side. The tools of the Draw tab act on it; see
[Tools](tools.md).

### Properties

The property grid: the members of the selection, by category, each editable in place. A row's menu holds the actions
of that member, such as Choose part on the Parts row. View ▸ Panels ▸ Properties shows it; Edit ▸ Properties of
selection moves the focus to it. See [Choose parts](../../how-to/choose-parts.md).

### Find

The find pane: search texts, tags and fields of the project, and replace them. Open it with Edit ▸ Find… or Edit ▸
Find and replace….

### Jobs

The jobs: the PDF exports that run in the background, with their progress and results. Open it with Output ▸ Jobs.
See [Reports and PDF](../../tutorials/reports-and-pdf.md).

### Preview

The preview of the open card: what a command, a quick fix or a paste would change, before you apply it.

### History

The undo steps of the project and the marker lines of exports and recoveries; undo to any step from here. Open it
with View ▸ Panels ▸ History.

### Glossary

The terms of EW with their EPLAN and QElectroTech names. Open it with Help ▸ Glossary. See
[EPLAN and EW](../../explanation/eplan-and-ew.md) and [QElectroTech and EW](../../explanation/qet-and-ew.md).

### Command bar

The tabs and groups of commands at the top of the window: Project, Page, Draw, Check, Output and View.
View ▸ Layout ▸ Collapse command bar folds it to its tab row.

### Strip editor

The Terminal strips navigator in table mode for one strip: insert, delete and move terminals, add accessories and
edit the cells of the terminal rows. Open it with Edit ▸ Strip editor with a strip selected. See
[A terminal strip and a cable](../../tutorials/terminal-strip-and-cable.md).

### Table mode

A navigator shown as a table: one row per record and a column per member, edited cell by cell; fill down, pattern
fill and paste work on several cells at once. The navigator's Table button switches it. Output ▸ Export table… and
Output ▸ Import table… exchange the table with a spreadsheet.

## Navigators

A navigator lists one kind of record of the project. View ▸ Panels ▸ Navigators shows each one; clicking a row
selects the record and opens its page.

### Pages

The pages in folio order, flat, by section or by structure.

### Devices

The devices with their functions and representations.

### Connections

The connections, the wires drawn on the pages.

### Terminal strips

The terminal strips with their terminals. Its table mode is the strip editor.

### Cables

The cables with their cores. See [A terminal strip and a cable](../../tutorials/terminal-strip-and-cable.md).

### PLC

The PLC configuration projects, stations, modules and channels, with their addresses.

### Potentials

The potentials with their definitions and members. See
[Define a supply potential](../../how-to/define-supply-potential.md).

### Interruption points

The interruption points grouped by name, with their partners.

### Structure identifiers

The function and location nodes of the project's structure.

### Parts in use

The parts the project uses, with their devices. See [Choose parts](../../how-to/choose-parts.md).

### Report blocks

The report blocks: the generated pages such as the terminal diagram, the cable list and the parts list. See
[Reports and PDF](../../tutorials/reports-and-pdf.md).

### Unplaced devices

The devices of each layout space that have no place on a rail or the plate yet.

### Macros

The macros of every library tier and of the project.

### Libraries

The installed libraries and the project's library copies with their state.

### Messages

The findings of the project's checks, by severity and rule. Check ▸ Next finding and Check ▸ Previous finding walk
them; Help ▸ Documentation on a finding opens its rule's page in the [rule reference](../rules/index.md). Open it with
Check ▸ Messages.
