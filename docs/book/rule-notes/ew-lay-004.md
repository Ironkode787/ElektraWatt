## What it checks

A placed device whose own location differs from the location of the layout space it is placed in, a device without a
location included. One finding per placement.

## Why

A device's location is where it is installed. Placing it in another location's cabinet contradicts its designation and
the reports grouped by location.

## Standard

No standard: a project-hygiene rule.

## Example

In a new project with two locations, place a device of one location in the other's layout.

## How to fix

The location is the device's `+` aspect in the property grid. There is no quick fix.

## Waiving

Waive it when the device is installed elsewhere on purpose and the designation stays as it is.
