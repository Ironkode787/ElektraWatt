## What it checks

A PELV potential without exactly one 0 V–PE bond, and a SELV potential with any. A 0 V potential is PELV when it or
one of its definitions says so, or a 0 V–PE bond ends at it. One finding per potential.

## Why

PELV and SELV are protective measures against electric shock. A PELV circuit is earthed at one point only: a second
bond, or a plain wire in place of the bond, puts protective-earth current through the 0 V conductors. A SELV circuit
must not be earthed at all.

## Standard

- IEC 60204-1:2016, 6.4 (unverified)
- IEC 60364-4-41:2005, 414 (unverified)

## Example

Golden A's 0V is PELV with the bond B1. In a copy, delete B1, or draw a second bond between 0V and PE.

## How to fix

Bonds are drawn with Draw ▸ Connect ▸ Bond and deleted like any connection. There is no quick fix.

## Waiving

This is a safety rule. Waive it only after an electrical review that records why the earthing is correct.
