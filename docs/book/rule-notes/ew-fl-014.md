## What it checks

A tube whose two ends lie in different locations, with no transition device between them (a bulkhead device ends a
tube). One finding per tube. Off by default.

## Why

A tube that leaves the cabinet passes through its wall; a bulkhead fitting keeps the cabinet's protection.

## Standard

No standard: a project-hygiene rule.

## Example

With the rule switched on, Golden A reports the 16 lines from the cabinet to the process valves in the field.

## How to fix

Add a bulkhead fitting device where the tube leaves the location. There is no quick fix.

## Waiving

Waive it when the cabinet has an open cable entry for its tubes.
