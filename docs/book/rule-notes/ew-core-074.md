## What it checks

Two drawn wires that each turn a corner on the same point without a junction there. One finding per pair of wires and
point, shown on the page.

## Why

Two corners on one point look like a joint: a reader takes the four arms for wires that are connected, or cannot tell
which arm belongs to which wire. A joint is drawn with a junction.

## Standard

No standard: a project-hygiene rule.

## Example

Draw a wire from the left that turns up at a point, then a second wire from the right that turns down at the same
point.

## How to fix

Select one of the wires and use Route, or drag one of its bends a grid step away, so that the two corners stand
apart. There is no quick fix.

## Waiving

Waive it only for a drawing copied from a source that must stay as it was drawn.
