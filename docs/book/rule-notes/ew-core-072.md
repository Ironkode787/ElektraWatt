## What it checks

A junction that wires leave in all four directions. One finding per junction, shown on the page.

## Why

A four-way junction is easily read as two wires crossing without a connection, above all where the dot is small or
the print is poor.

## Standard

No standard: a project-hygiene rule.

## Example

Draw a wire down through a junction, then a second wire into it from the left and a third from the right.

## How to fix

Move one branch to a junction of its own a grid step away, so that each junction joins three wires. The router never
splits a junction: a junction is connectivity. There is no quick fix.

## Waiving

Waive it only for a drawing copied from a source that must stay as it was drawn.
