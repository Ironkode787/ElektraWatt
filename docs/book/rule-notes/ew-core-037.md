## What it checks

A cable shield that no connection assigns, so it is connected at neither end. Only a cable wired core by core is
judged; a cable drawn as a whole has no modelled shield connection. A shield laid on at one end only counts as
connected. One finding per shield; online only.

## Why

A shield works only when it is connected, usually to earth at one or both ends. A shield left open gives no screening.

## Standard

No standard: a project-hygiene rule.

## Example

In a new project, wire a shielded cable's cores and leave its shield conductor unassigned.

## How to fix

Draw a core connection from the shield to the earthing point. There is no quick fix.

## Waiving

Waive it when the shield is connected on site by a gland or clamp that the drawing does not show.
