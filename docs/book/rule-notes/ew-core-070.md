## What it checks

A drawn wire whose route passes through a text the page prints: a device tag, a slot or pin text, a cross-reference,
a free text or a structure box's caption. One finding per wire and owner of the text, shown on the page; the message
quotes the text.

## Why

A line through a text makes the text hard to read and can change how it reads, a tag or a cross-reference above all.

## Standard

No standard: a project-hygiene rule.

## Example

Draw a wire whose route runs horizontally through a coil's tag.

## How to fix

Select the wire and use Route selection, or move the text. There is no quick fix.

## Waiving

Waive it only when the text cannot move and the overlap leaves it readable.
