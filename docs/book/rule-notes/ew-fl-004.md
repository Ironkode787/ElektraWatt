## What it checks

Each position of a valve terminal that holds a spare valve or a blanking plate. One finding per position, an info; it
runs in release mode too.

## Why

Spare and blank positions are capacity for later use. Listing them lets the reviewer check that the reserve is what
was ordered.

## Standard

No standard: a project-hygiene rule.

## Example

On Golden A, 20 valve positions are spare.

## How to fix

Nothing to fix when the reserve is intended. There is no quick fix.

## Waiving

Waive it when the spare positions are agreed; the finding is an info.
