## What it checks

The pieces of one physical wire (the connections that EW joins into one wire across interruption points and junctions)
that name different conductors or carry different overrides. The value of the piece that carries the wire applies. One
finding per physical wire.

## Why

One physical wire has one cross-section, one colour and one core. Different values on its pieces mean the drawing
disagrees with itself, and the wire list shows only one of them.

## Standard

No standard: a project-hygiene rule.

## Example

In a new project, draw a wire through an interruption point and give the two legs different cross-sections.

## How to fix

The finding names the physical wire; its pieces are listed in the property grid. There is no quick fix.

## Waiving

Waive it only when the value of the carrying piece is the right one and the others cannot be cleared.
