## What it checks

A bus port connected by a cable whose part is made for other bus systems, and two ports mated without a cable whose
parts name different connector types.

## Why

A cable for another bus, or a connector that does not fit, breaks the line.

## Standard

No standard: the cable and connector types are the parts'.

## Example

Connect a PROFINET port with a cable part made for PROFIBUS.

## How to fix

Use a bus cable for the port's bus, and connectors of one type. There is no quick fix.

## Waiving

Waive it for an adapter that the parts do not show, with the reason.
