## What it checks

The table of contents whose page count did not settle: its confirming round needed more pages than the first estimate
gave, so the larger count is used and the last page of each table-of-contents block takes every remaining row. One
finding.

## Why

The table of contents lists pages that come after it, and its own length shifts their numbers. When the count does not
settle, its last page may be overfull.

## Standard

No standard: a project-hygiene rule.

## Example

It needs a table of contents whose entries sit right at a page boundary; Golden A has none.

## How to fix

Page descriptions are the usual long entries. There is no quick fix.

## Waiving

Waive it when the overfull last page prints acceptably.
