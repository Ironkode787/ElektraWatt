## What it checks

A tube whose pressure rating is below the highest nominal pressure its net carries, or whose outer diameter differs
from the push-in size of the port at either end. One finding per misfit.

## Why

A tube rated below its pressure can burst; a 6 mm tube does not hold in an 8 mm push-in port.

## Standard

No standard: a project-hygiene rule.

## Example

A tube part rated 5 bar on a line that the medium "compressed air 6 bar" reaches.

## How to fix

Choose another tube part, or set the tube on the line. There is no quick fix.

## Waiving

Waive it when the part data are wrong and the tube is known to fit.
