## What it checks

A tube whose effective specification lacks the outer or inner diameter or the material: neither the line, its tube
part, the medium of its net nor the project's default tube gives them. One finding per tube.

## Why

A tube without a size cannot be cut, ordered or labelled.

## Standard

No standard: a project-hygiene rule.

## Example

On Golden A, the 16 working lines F1 to F16 carry no tube specification.

## How to fix

Give the medium or the project a default tube, or set the tube on the lines. There is no quick fix.

## Waiving

Waive it for a line that a supplier makes up, such as a hose that comes with a machine.
