## What it checks

A wire whose effective colour is green-yellow (GNYE) on a potential whose type is neither PE nor PEN. Bond wires are
exempt: they take the bond's colour. One finding per wire.

## Why

Green-yellow is reserved for protective conductors. A green-yellow wire that carries anything else will be taken for
earth by whoever works on the machine.

## Standard

- IEC 60204-1:2016, 13.2.2

## Example

In a copy of Golden A, give a 24 V wire the colour GNYE in the property grid.

## How to fix

Give the wire another colour, or move it to the protective-earth potential if it is a protective conductor.

## Waiving

This is a safety rule. Do not waive it; there is no case where a live conductor may be green-yellow.
