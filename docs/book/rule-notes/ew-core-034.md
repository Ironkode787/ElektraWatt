## What it checks

A wired external connection point (an interruption point marked external) whose wire is not a cable core. The external
points of one page are grouped by the external location: one finding per page and location, listing the inside devices
and the wires. A point that is not wired is left to EW-CORE-051.

## Why

A wire that leaves the cabinet runs in a cable. Without the cable, the cable list misses it and nobody orders or
labels it.

## Standard

No standard: a project-hygiene rule.

## Example

On Golden A, the supply from +J-E-206 to 1X1 (wires W1 to W3) has no cable.

## How to fix

Draw the cable over the external wires and assign a core to each. There is no quick fix.

## Waiving

Waive it when the connection is made by others, for example the customer's supply cable, and say so.
