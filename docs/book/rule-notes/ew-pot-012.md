## What it checks

The connections of a potential that form a ring in the drawing. EW leaves the last connection of the ring out of the
physical wires and reports it. One finding per ring; online only.

## Why

A ring drawn on a page usually means one wire too many. The physical wires and the wire list drop one connection of
the ring, which may not be the one you expect.

## Standard

No standard: a project-hygiene rule.

## Example

In a new project, draw three wires between three connection points of one potential so that they close a loop.

## How to fix

The finding names the connection that EW leaves out of the wires. There is no quick fix.

## Waiving

Waive it when the ring is intended, for example a ring-fed PE bar, and the wire that EW leaves out is the right one.
