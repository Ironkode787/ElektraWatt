## What it checks

A channel with a manual address or a symbol that reaches nothing, while nobody marked it spare, reserved or
deactivated.

## Why

An address or a symbol on an unconnected channel is a signal that was planned and never wired.

## Standard

No standard: a completeness rule.

## Example

Give a channel without a binding the symbol Spare_1.

## How to fix

Connect the channel, or set its state to spare or reserved. There is no quick fix.

## Waiving

Waive it when the channel is kept for a later extension, with the reason.
