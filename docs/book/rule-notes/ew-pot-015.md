## What it checks

One wire number printed on two or more physical wires. With the shipped numbering scheme each physical wire has its
own number, so a finding needs stored or frozen numbers; numbering schemes with other units arrive later. One finding
per number, naming its wires.

## Why

A wire number identifies a conductor at both ends. Two wires with one number cannot be told apart on site or in the
wire list.

## Standard

- IEC 60204-1:2016, 13.2.1

## Example

Golden A has none. In a copy, freeze two wires to the same number.

## How to fix

The frozen flag is on the wire's number row in the property grid. There is no quick fix.

## Waiving

Waive it only when the two wires are joined on site and the documentation says so.
