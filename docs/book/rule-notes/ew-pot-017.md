## What it checks

Two or more potential definitions with different names in one potential. The first name in natural order names the
potential. One finding per potential, naming the definitions.

## Why

A potential has one name on the drawing, the wire list and the interruption points. Two names for one node mean the
drawing joins what the designer meant to keep apart, or one name is a typing error.

## Standard

No standard: a project-hygiene rule.

## Example

In a new project, define two potentials with different names on two ends of one wire.

## How to fix

The potentials navigator lists the definitions of each potential. There is no quick fix.

## Waiving

Waive it when the second name is a deliberate alias and the first name is the one to print.
