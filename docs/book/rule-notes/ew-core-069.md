## What it checks

A drawn wire whose route passes through the body of a symbol or a black box, other than where it leaves one of its
own connection points. One finding per wire and symbol, shown on the page.

## Why

A wire drawn through a symbol hides part of it and reads as passing through the device.

## Standard

No standard: a project-hygiene rule.

## Example

Place a contactor between two terminals and draw a straight wire from one terminal to the other across it.

## How to fix

Select the wire and use Route selection, or move the symbol out of the wire's way. There is no quick fix.

## Waiving

Waive it only when the symbol is meant to be drawn over the line, and a note on the page says so.
