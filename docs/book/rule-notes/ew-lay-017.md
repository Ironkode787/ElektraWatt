## What it checks

A device placed on a surface itself (a door, a side wall, the top, the bottom or the mounting plate) whose part goes
in a cut-out or is screwed on but has no holes in the part editor's Front view and holes, once per part with the
devices that use it; and a device placed there without a part. A device on a rail needs no holes and is never named.

## Why

The hole table and the drawings for drilling list only the holes the program knows: a part without its holes leaves
its cut-out or its fixing holes out of them, and the shop finds out at the door.

## Standard

No standard: a project-hygiene rule.

## Example

Make a pilot lamp part of your own without holes and place a device of it on the door's page.

## How to fix

Give the part its holes in the part editor (Front view and holes), or give the device its part. There is no quick
fix.

## Waiving

Waive it for a part whose holes the shop makes from the maker's own drawing.
