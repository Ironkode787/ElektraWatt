## What it checks

A placement or carrier that does not fit its carrier or surface. Placements: on a duct, beside or turned on a rail or
past its ends, off the plate or turned other than by 90°, a part whose mounting does not fit the rail's profile, a
footprint past the rail's ends or outside the plate's usable area. Carriers: a rail or duct of a shape EW cannot use,
or outside the usable area. Also the limits of this release: one layout space per location, one enclosure per space
and one plate per enclosure. And a strip's end bracket that overlaps another placement or leaves the rail.

## Why

A device that does not fit its rail or plate cannot be mounted as drawn, and lengths and the material list built on
the layout are wrong.

## Standard

No standard: a project-hygiene rule.

## Example

In a new project, add terminals to a strip until it grows past the end of its rail.

## How to fix

For the limits of this release, remove the extra layout space, enclosure or plate. There is no quick fix.

## Waiving

Do not waive it for the release limits; for a fit problem, waive it only after a check on the real panel.
