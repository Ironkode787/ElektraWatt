## What it checks

A bridge of a terminal strip without a part: the parts list and the assembly list cannot name what the fitter pushes
into the terminals. One finding per bridge, named as the lists name it (BR1, BR2, …).

## Why

A bridge joins terminals of one potential; the wrong bridge, or none, leaves a terminal unfed or two potentials joined.
The part says how many poles it has and which terminals it fits. A release names every part it orders, so the release
check makes it an error.

## Standard

No standard: a project-hygiene rule.

## Example

Bridge terminals 1 to 4 of -X1021 and give the bridge no part: the bridge BR1 of -X1021 (terminals 1 to 4) has no part.

## How to fix

Choose a part in the strip editor's Bridge part cell of one of the bridge's terminals. There is no quick fix.

## Waiving

Waive it for a bridge the terminals bring with them, which nobody orders on its own.
