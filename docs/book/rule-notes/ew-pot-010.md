## What it checks

A clamp on which more conductors land than its part says it takes. Two wires of one size that share a twin ferrule
count as one conductor. A clamp whose part names no capacity is not judged. One finding per clamp.

## Why

A clamp holds the conductors it is made for; one more is not held safely.

## Standard

- IEC 60204-1:2016, 13.1.1 (unverified)

## Example

In a copy of the sample project, set Project properties ▸ Workshop ▸ Two wires in one clamp to a ferrule each: three
ferrules then land on clamp a of the PE terminal -X1011:PE, which takes two.

## How to fix

Land a wire on another clamp or a further terminal, or give two wires of one size a twin ferrule where the clamp takes
one. There is no quick fix.

## Waiving

Waive it only where the part's data is wrong, and correct the part.
