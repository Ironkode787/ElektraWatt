## What it checks

A drawn page whose folio number lies outside the folio range of its section. A page without a section (front matter)
has no range. One finding per page, shown on the page.

## Why

Sections reserve folio ranges so that a page's number says where it belongs. A page outside its section's range breaks
that promise, and a later page of the right section may collide with it.

## Standard

No standard: a project-hygiene rule.

## Example

In a copy of Golden A, renumber a page of a section to a folio beyond the section's range.

## How to fix

Change the page's folio in its properties, or move the page in the page navigator. There is no quick fix.

## Waiving

Waive it when the page belongs to the section on purpose and the folio is fixed by the customer's numbering.
