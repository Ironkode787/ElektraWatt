## What it checks

A core of a cable whose part defines its cores, where the core differs from the part's core of the same
identification: its colour, cross-section, shield flag or pair, or an identification the part does not define. A cable
without such a part is not judged. One finding per core.

## Why

The cable part says what the real cable holds. A core that differs is drawn with a colour or size the cable does not
have, so the cable list and the wiring disagree.

## Standard

No standard: a project-hygiene rule.

## Example

In a copy of Golden A, change the colour of a core of a cable whose part defines its cores.

## How to fix

The cable part's cores are listed in the part's copy in the project. There is no quick fix.

## Waiving

Waive it for a cable that is made to order with cores that differ from the catalogue part.
