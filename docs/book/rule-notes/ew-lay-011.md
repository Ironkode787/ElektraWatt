## What it checks

The stored ways of wires that no longer hold: an end's device moved, turned or is no longer placed, an end moved to
another pin, or a duct on the way moved, changed or is gone. One finding per cause, with its wires as targets; their
lengths print with "≈" until Route lays them again. A warning, an error in release mode.

## Why

A length cut along a way that no longer exists is too short or too long in the cabinet.

## Standard

No standard: a project-hygiene rule.

## Example

Run Route on the panel page, then turn a terminal strip on its rail.

## How to fix

Run Route on the panel page (the quick fix): it lays the wires whose ways no longer hold again.

## Waiving

Waive it only when the lengths are typed by hand and the ways do not matter.
