## What it checks

A symbol with connection points, or a drawn box, whose body lies on another one, or that stands partly outside the
page's drawing area. One finding per pair of symbols and per symbol outside, shown on the page. A symbol drawn on
another one's connection point (an interruption point on a pin) is not reported, nor are the valves in a valve
terminal's cells.

## Why

Symbols on each other cannot be told apart, and their wires cannot be drawn clear of them. A symbol outside the
drawing area runs into the frame and may be cut off in print.

## Standard

No standard: a project-hygiene rule.

## Example

Place a contactor so that its body covers part of a breaker above it.

## How to fix

Drag one of the symbols to a free place, or into the frame. There is no quick fix.

## Waiving

Waive it only for a drawing copied from a source that must stay as it was drawn.
