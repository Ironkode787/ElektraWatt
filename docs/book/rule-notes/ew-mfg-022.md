## What it checks

A wire whose part, the stock it is cut from, is of another cross-section or colour than the wire itself. The part is
the one the wire names, its potential's definition names or the project's default names. One finding per wire.

## Why

A wire's size and colour come from the project's colour and size rules and the drawing shows them. A shop that cut
the wire from its part would cut the wrong size or the wrong colour. Every list prints the wire's own size and colour.

## Standard

No standard: a project-hygiene rule.

## Example

Give the wires of the 24 V potential the part of a 1.5 mm² black wire while the size rules make them 0.75 mm² blue.

## How to fix

Give the wire a part of its own size and colour, or leave its part empty: a wire without a part takes the first wire
part of the workshop stock of its size and colour. There is no quick fix.

## Waiving

Waive it for a wire the shop cuts from a larger stock on purpose.
