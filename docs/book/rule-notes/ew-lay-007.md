## What it checks

A footprint or a duct that stands inside the room a device's part keeps free: the device's footprint grown by its
part's clearance above, below, left and right, turned with the part. One finding per device and intruder, naming the
side and how much too close it stands. The device's own rail and the end brackets are not judged, nor a device whose
footprint is not known.

## Why

A maker asks for room around a device for its heat, an arc or a hand. A device placed closer by hand, or a part
changed later, takes that room away without a word.

## Standard

No standard: a project-hygiene rule.

## Example

Give a power supply's part a clearance of 100 mm below it in the part editor (Size and mounting), with a duct 75 mm
below the device on the plate.

## How to fix

Move the device or the duct until the room is free, or correct the part's clearance where it is wrong. There is no
quick fix.

## Waiving

Waive it when the maker allows the closer place for this cabinet.
