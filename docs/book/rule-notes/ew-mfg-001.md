## What it checks

In a project with a panel layout, the wires of a cabinet that Route lays and that have no length: no way stored and
none typed. One finding per cabinet, with the wires as its targets. A field wire and a cable's core are not counted:
the site and the cable give their lengths. A stale length is EW-LAY-011's.

## Why

The wire list, the cut list and the consumables need a length for every wire the shop cuts.

## Standard

No standard: a project-hygiene rule.

## Example

Place a cabinet's devices and draw its ducts, then check the project before running Route.

## How to fix

Run Route on the cabinet's panel page (the quick fix), or type a wire's length in the Connections table.

## Waiving

Waive it when the fitter measures the wires in the cabinet.
