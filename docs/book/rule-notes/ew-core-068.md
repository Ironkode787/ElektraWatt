## What it checks

Two drawn wires with a stretch of positive length on one line. One finding per pair of wires, shown on the page. The
stub of a connection point that both wires end at is theirs to share and does not count.

## Why

A reader cannot tell two wires on one line apart: the page shows one wire where the model has two, and where they
part it is unclear which goes where.

## Standard

No standard: a project-hygiene rule.

## Example

Draw two wires whose routes run along the same horizontal line for a few grid steps.

## How to fix

Select one of the wires and use Route selection, so that each runs on a line of its own. There is no quick fix.

## Waiving

Waive it only for a drawing copied from a source that must stay as it was drawn.
