## What it checks

A wire end that cannot be made as drawn: a fine-stranded wire into an insulation-displacement clamp, which takes solid
and stranded wire only. A wire whose part names no conductor class is taken as fine-stranded, the common panel wire.
One finding per end.

## Why

The rule table that gives each end its treatment (a ferrule, a twin ferrule, a ring lug, bare) has no row for it, and
the wire list prints `?` in its end cell. The fitter would stop at that end.

## Standard

No standard: a project-hygiene rule.

## Example

Give a terminal part an insulation-displacement clamp in the part editor and land a wire on it.

## How to fix

Give the wire a part of a solid or stranded wire, or land it on a clamp that takes fine strands. There is no quick fix.

## Waiving

Waive it where the shop makes such an end on purpose, and say how.
