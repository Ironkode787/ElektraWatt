## What it checks

Two or more devices that print the same full designation, compared under the project's case setting. Terminals are
left out (EW-TERM-002), and so are unnumbered designations. One finding per designation, with the fix "Renumber the
newer". The check runs over the whole project; an edit is not refused for it.

## Why

A reference designation identifies one object. Two devices with one designation make cross-references, the parts list
and the labels ambiguous.

## Standard

- IEC 81346-1:2022, 6 (unverified)

## Example

In a copy of Golden A, freeze a device's tag to the tag of another device.

## How to fix

Renumber the newer device with the quick fix, or give one of the devices another designation.

## Waiving

Do not waive it: two objects never share a designation.
