## What it checks

A text the program places that lies on another symbol than its own. One finding per text and symbol, shown on the
page; the message quotes the text.

## Why

A text on a symbol hides the symbol's lines and reads as that symbol's text. A pin's number where the symbol's author
put it is not reported: it belongs to its pin.

## Standard

No standard: a project-hygiene rule.

## Example

Place a lamp so close beside a contact that the contact's tag can stand nowhere but on the lamp.

## How to fix

Drag the text to a free place, or move the symbol it lies on. There is no quick fix.

## Waiving

Waive it only when the text stays readable and clearly belongs to its own symbol.
