## What it checks

A mountable device of a location that has a layout space, without a placement in it. A location without a layout space
reports nothing. One finding per device: a warning online, an error in release mode.

## Why

Once a cabinet has a layout, every device of it must have a place on a rail or the plate; otherwise the panel builder
does not know where it goes.

## Standard

No standard: a project-hygiene rule.

## Example

On Golden A, F55, E55, E56, S56 and -201U9 are not placed.

## How to fix

Place the device from the device navigator onto the layout page. There is no quick fix.

## Waiving

Waive it for a device that is mounted outside the layout, such as a door device not drawn yet.
