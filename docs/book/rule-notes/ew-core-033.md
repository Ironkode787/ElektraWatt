## What it checks

A cable without a length: neither a manual length, nor a routed one, nor one from the cable part. One finding per
cable. It blocks release. A length taken from the part is not checked further yet.

## Why

A cable without a length cannot be cut, ordered or priced, and the cable list is incomplete.

## Standard

No standard: a project-hygiene rule.

## Example

On Golden A, -3W1 has no length.

## How to fix

The length is a property of the cable. There is no quick fix.

## Waiving

Waive it only when the cable is supplied and cut by others, and name them in the reason.
