## What it checks

The holes of a surface (the mounting plate, a door, a side wall, the top or the bottom), as the hole table lists
them: two holes whose edges are closer than the workshop's web between holes (5 mm when the project sets none, the program's assumption), a
hole closer to the sheet's edge than its edge margin and that web, and a hole whose centre lies off the sheet.

## Why

A web of sheet metal that is too thin tears when the second hole is punched, and a cut-out in the edge margin meets
the door's fold or its seal: the holes cannot be made where they are drawn.

## Standard

No standard: a project-hygiene rule.

## Example

Place two pilot lamps on a door's page with their centres 25 mm apart: their 22.3 mm cut-outs leave less than 5 mm
between them.

## How to fix

Move a device, a hole by hand or a carrier, or move one hole of its pattern in its Properties (Holes). Set the web
the shop keeps in Project properties ▸ Workshop ▸ Web between holes. There is no quick fix.

## Waiving

Waive it only after the shop has checked that the sheet takes the holes as drawn.
