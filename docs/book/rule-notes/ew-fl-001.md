## What it checks

A placed valve function, of a valve that is fitted, whose working port 2 or 4 has no line to another device and no
closure (a plug, a silencer or a blanking). One finding per valve function, listing the open ports.

## Why

An open working port blows air or oil into the cabinet. A port left open on purpose should be closed or marked.

## Standard

No standard: a project-hygiene rule.

## Example

On Golden A, the 20 spare valves leave ports 2 and 4 open.

## How to fix

The closure is a property of the port. There is no quick fix.

## Waiving

Waive it for a spare valve whose ports are plugged by the manufacturer; Golden A's spares are a case.
