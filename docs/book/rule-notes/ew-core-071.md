## What it checks

A graphic line or polyline whose end lies exactly on a connection point of the same page. One finding per line and
connection point, shown on the page; online only. There is no quick fix: converting the line takes two commands.

## Why

A graphic line has no electrical meaning. Drawn onto a pin it looks like a wire but joins nothing, a habit from
QElectroTech drawings.

## Standard

No standard: a project-hygiene rule.

## Example

In a new project, draw a graphic line from a free point to a device's pin.

## How to fix

Delete the line and draw a connection in its place. There is no quick fix: converting takes two commands.

## Waiving

Waive it when the line is a deliberate drawing aid, such as a mechanical linkage, that only touches the pin.
