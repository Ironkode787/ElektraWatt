## What it checks

A fluid net that two different media reach directly: two medium definition points, or a definition and the outlet of
a regulator. One finding per net.

## Why

One tube cannot carry 6 bar and 4 bar at once. Two definitions in one net mean that a line joins two supplies that
should be apart.

## Standard

No standard: a project-hygiene rule.

## Example

Join the 6 bar supply line to the outlet line of a regulator set to 4 bar.

## How to fix

Remove one of the medium definitions, or separate the lines. There is no quick fix.

## Waiving

Waive it only when the two media are one in practice, such as two names for the same supply.
