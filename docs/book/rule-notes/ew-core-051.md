## What it checks

An interruption point without a partner of the same name, or one that is not external but has no connection point, so
it can pair with nothing. An external point counts as paired. One finding per point; the name is not in the
fingerprint, so renaming keeps a waiver.

## Why

An interruption point says "this potential continues there". Without a partner the reader cannot follow the circuit,
and the potential stops at the page edge.

## Standard

- IEC 61082-1:2014, Figure 9 (unverified)

## Example

In a new project, place one interruption point named 24V and no other point of that name.

## How to fix

For a connection that leaves the project, mark the point external instead. There is no quick fix.

## Waiving

Do not waive it for a release; pair or remove the point.
