## What it checks

A fluid net whose ports all have exhaust roles (3, 5, 82, 84), or that carries exhaust, with no collector line, no
closure and no port of another role. A gallery of a valve terminal is one finding. Off by default.

## Why

An open exhaust is loud and blows oil mist into the cabinet; a silencer or a collector line is usually wanted.

## Standard

No standard: a project-hygiene rule.

## Example

With the rule switched on, Golden A reports galleries 3 and 5 of the valve terminal -201U1.

## How to fix

Close the exhaust with a silencer, or draw a collector line. There is no quick fix.

## Waiving

Waive it for an exhaust that vents on purpose, such as a valve with built-in silencers.
