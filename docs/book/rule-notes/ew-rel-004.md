## What it checks

A revision label that does not follow the project's label format, or does not come after every earlier label. It runs
in the gate; the commands refuse such a label when it is set.

## Why

Revision labels show the order of the issues of a drawing. A label out of order makes the newest issue look older.

## Standard

No standard: a project-hygiene rule.

## Example

Open a project file whose second revision has the label A while the first has B.

## How to fix

There is no quick fix.

## Waiving

Release-gate findings cannot be waived.
