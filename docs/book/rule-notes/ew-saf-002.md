## What it checks

A 1oo2 channel without a partner that names it back, and a complete pair whose channels reach two different devices.

## Why

A two-channel evaluation needs both contacts of one sensor; two sensors defeat it.

## Standard

- ISO 13849-1:2023 (unverified)

## Example

Pair two channels as 1oo2 and wire them to two emergency-stop buttons.

## How to fix

Pair the channels and wire both to the one two-channel sensor. There is no quick fix.

## Waiving

Do not waive it: the safety function is not what it claims.
