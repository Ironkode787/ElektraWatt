The check does not judge the workshop stock. What the files lack of the stock and of its parts' data:
  No ring lug of the workshop stock takes 2.5 mm²: those ends are listed without a part.
  No wire part of the workshop stock matches 0.75 mm² BK, 0.75 mm² BU, 0.75 mm² DBU, 0.75 mm² GNYE, 1.5 mm² BK, 1.5 mm² BU, 2.5 mm² BK, 2.5 mm² BU, 2.5 mm² DBU, 2.5 mm² GNYE: the lists name no part for those wires.
  Cable markers: no marker part in the stock marks cables; the label program asks which to print on.

How to read these papers
  Length mm is the length to cut: routed, the way along the ducts with 50 mm at each end and 300 mm across a door's hinge; typed, as typed.
  A length of — is none: the fitter measures the wire.
  A wire's marker names its far end. Cable cores and wires with both ends on one device carry none.
  On a terminal Side 1 (clamp a) is the cabinet's wiring and Side 2 (clamp b) the field.
  A wire with an end in another location is cut, laid and landed on site, at its cabinet end too: the cut list leaves it out.
  Work in this order: drill (drawings for drilling, hole table), mount (assembly lists), cut (cut list), wire (wire list), mark (label files).
