  No wire part of the workshop stock matches 0.75 mm² BK, 0.75 mm² BU, 0.75 mm² DBU, 0.75 mm² GNYE, 1.5 mm² BK, 1.5 mm² BU, 2.5 mm² BK, 2.5 mm² BU, 2.5 mm² DBU, 2.5 mm² GNYE: the lists name no part for those wires.
  Cable markers: no marker part in the stock marks cables; the label program asks which to print on
